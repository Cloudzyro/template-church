# Unslop pass

Done with the unslop skill (by Damir Sokolovsky, @heydamir, CC BY 4.0), 24 November 2026.

## Audit

Slop level: medium. Warm and specific underneath (forest, gold and clay, Young Serif, a chapel illustration), with a layer of template habits on top.

- Uppercase tracked labels: section eyebrows, service-time days, event months and weekdays, the step labels on Plan a visit, the sermon series, the logo's "Community Church", footer headings
- Radial gold washes behind the home hero, the sermon poster (two blobs) and the giving box
- Decorative orb on every page hero: a gold disc in a green disc inside a dotted ring (`PageHero.astro`)
- The hero illustration clipped to an organic blob, with a gold halo around the sun and a colored drop shadow
- Looping motion: the cloud drifting forever (`HeroArt.astro`)
- Feature icons boxed in dark green tiles with gold icons (home, Plan a visit, Ministries), 52 to 60px
- Equal card grids with a checkerboard of alternating sand backgrounds (home "Welcome home" cards, Ministries)
- Pill buttons everywhere, lifting 2px on hover, the primary with a green colored shadow; nav links, play buttons and form choices as pills too
- Weights 700 and 800 on labels, names, links and summaries
- Trailing periods on headlines ("There's a seat saved for you.", "This page wandered off.")
- A generic headline on giving ("Generosity changes lives")
- A missing space in "or send us a note" on Plan a visit

## Direction

- Product: the website of a neighborhood church, so newcomers can plan a first Sunday, watch a sermon, find an event or a ministry, and give
- Character: neighborly, plainspoken, steady
- World: a white chapel on a cedar-lined lane, a fellowship hall with folding tables and a coffee urn
- Moments: the chapel on the hill in the hero, framed in an arched window, and the service times band with Sunday 9am in gold
- Signature: the short clay rule before each section label

## What changed

- Spacing: "Welcome home" is one card holding four rows with a fixed icon column instead of a 2 by 2 grid; ministries on the home page sit under hairlines instead of in boxes
- Color: all washes, the halo and the colored shadows removed; one gold highlight per screen (the 9am service, the Give button); no checkerboard backgrounds
- Icons: one line set at 22px in the forest green text color, no colored tiles
- Copy: no periods on headlines; giving is "Where your giving goes"; the "or send us a note" space fixed
- Type: every label in sentence case at normal tracking; weights 400 to 600
- Layout and visuals: the hero illustration is framed as an arched window instead of a blob; page heroes are plain forest without the orb
- Details: buttons at a 12px radius, play buttons and choices at 10px, nav links at 8px; event date tiles at 12px; large rounded bands at 28px instead of 40px
- Motion: the cloud stands still; buttons change color on hover and press down slightly; one easing curve
